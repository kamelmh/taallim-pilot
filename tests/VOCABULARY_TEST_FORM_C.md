## Scoring Guide

| Section | Items | Marks | Scoring |
|---------|-------|-------|---------|
| A: Picture matching | 1–10 | 10 | 1 mark per correct match |
| B: MCQ (meaning) | 11–25 | 15 | 1 mark per correct answer |
| C: Fill-blank | 26–40 | 15 | 1 mark per correct word (spelling must match) |
| D: Translation | 41–50 | 10 | 1 mark per correct translation (accept listed variants) |
| E: Sentence use | 51–60 | 10 | 0.5 meaning + 0.5 grammar (max 1 per item) |
| **Total** | **60** | **60** | Convert to %: (score ÷ 60) × 100 |

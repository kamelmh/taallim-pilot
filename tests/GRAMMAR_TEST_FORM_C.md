> **Level:** Middle School (1AM–4AM)
> **Duration:** 60 minutes
> **Total Items:** 60
> **Total Marks:** 55 (5 buffer items, not scored)
> **Note:** Tests the SAME 60 structures as Forms A and B, with different lexical content

---

## Instructions for Students

**Name:** ________________________________ **Class:** ________________

**Date:** ________________ **Group:** □ Experimental □ Control

**Time allowed: 60 minutes**

This test has four sections. Answer ALL questions.

- **Section A:** Multiple Choice — choose the correct answer (A, B, C, or D)
- **Section B:** Fill in the Blank — write the correct word(s) in the gap
- **Section C:** Error Correction — find ONE error in each sentence, underline it, and write the correction
- **Section D:** Sentence Building — rearrange the words to form a correct sentence

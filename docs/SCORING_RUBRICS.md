# Ta'allim Pilot — Scoring Rubrics

> **Purpose:** Ensure inter-rater reliability across teachers scoring the same student responses.
> **Usage:** Each scorer reads these rubrics before scoring. Discrepancies > 1 point per item require discussion.

---

## Section C: Error Correction (Items 36–45)

**Total: 10 marks (0.5 per error identification + 0.5 per correction)**

### What students must do
1. **Underline** the ONE error in each sentence
2. **Write** the correct form on the line provided

### Scoring criteria

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **1.0** | Error correctly identified (underlined) AND correction is accurate |
| **0.5** | Error correctly identified but correction is wrong/missing |
| **0.5** | Error not identified but correction is the exact correct form for the sentence |
| **0.0** | Neither identification nor correction is correct, OR two errors identified (task says ONE) |

### Partial credit rules

| Scenario | Score | Rationale |
|----------|-------|-----------|
| Underlines wrong word, writes wrong correction | 0 | No evidence of understanding |
| Underlines wrong word, writes correct correction by accident | 0.5 | Correction is correct but misidentified |
| Underlines correct error, writes "doesn't" for "don't" (spelling error) | 0.5 + 0.5 = 1.0 | Spelling variant accepted if phonetically close |
| Underlines correct error, writes entirely wrong word | 0.5 | Identified error but can't correct |
| Underlines two words | 0 | Task explicitly says "ONE error" |
| Writes correction but doesn't underline | 0.5 | Corrected but didn't identify |
| Writes correct answer in wrong item slot | 0 | Answer belongs to different item |

### Accepted spelling variants

For items where multiple correct forms exist:

| Item | Primary answer | Accept also |
|------|---------------|-------------|
| 36 (don't→doesn't) | doesn't | doesn't |
| 37 (play→plays) | plays | plays |
| 41 (went→go) | go | — |
| 42 (drives→drive) | drive | — |
| 43 (are→is) | is | — |
| 44 (cans→can) | can | — |
| 45 (is→are) | are | — |

### Inter-rater protocol
1. Each teacher scores independently
2. Compare scores for items where identification and correction differ
3. If scores differ by > 0.5 on any item, discuss and agree
4. Final scores recorded in ` scoring_log.csv`

## Section D: Sentence Building (Items 46–55)

**Total: 10 marks (1 mark per item)**

### What students must do
Rearrange the given words to form a correct English sentence, adding capitalization and punctuation.

### Scoring criteria

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **1.0** | Correct word order + correct capitalization + correct punctuation |
| **0.75** | Correct word order + correct capitalization, but missing/wrong punctuation |
| **0.5** | Correct word order but missing capitalization AND punctuation |
| **0.25** | Mostly correct word order (1 word out of place) but capitalization/punctuation correct |
| **0.0** | Word order is wrong, OR sentence is unreadable, OR left blank |

### Capitalization rules
- First word of sentence MUST be capitalized
- Proper nouns (Algiers, Constantine, etc.) MUST be capitalized
- "I" MUST be capitalized

### Punctuation rules
- Period (.) at end of declarative sentences
- Question mark (?) at end of interrogative sentences
- Exclamation mark (!) acceptable for exclamatory sentences

### Partial credit detail

| Scenario | Score |
|----------|-------|
| "he goes to school every day." (missing capital H) | 0.5 |
| "He goes to school every day" (missing period) | 0.75 |
| "He goes to school every day." (perfect) | 1.0 |
| "He go to school every day." (verb error, not word order) | 0.5 |
| "To school he goes every day." (word order wrong) | 0.0 |
| "He goes to school every day." + extra words added | 0.0 |
| Blank / unreadable | 0.0 |

### Word order acceptability
Some items have acceptable variations:

| Item | Standard answer | Accept also |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| 46 | He goes to school every day. | Every day he goes to school. |
| 51 | How is the weather today? | How is the weather today? |
| 52 | What does your mother do? | What does your mother do? |
| 53 | I go to school every day. | Every day I go to school. |

### Inter-rater protocol
1. Score word order first (0 or 1)
2. Check capitalization (deduct 0.25 if missing)
3. Check punctuation (deduct 0.25 if missing)
4. If scores differ by > 0.25 on any item, discuss and agree

## General Scoring Notes

### Converting raw scores to percentages
- **Grammar:** (score ÷ 55) × 100
- **Vocabulary:** (score ÷ 60) × 100

### Floor/ceiling effects
- If > 90% of students score > 90%, items may be too easy
- If > 50% of students score < 30%, items may be too difficult

### Missing responses
- Blank = 0 marks
- "Don't know" or "DK" = 0 marks
- Illegible = 0 marks (note on scoring sheet)

---

## Scorer Training Checklist

Before scoring begins, each teacher must:

- [ ] Read these rubrics completely
- [ ] Score 5 practice papers (not from the pilot) together as a group
- [ ] Achieve > 90% agreement on practice papers
- [ ] Sign the scorer agreement form
